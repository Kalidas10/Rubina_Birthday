Act II • Our Golden Adventure

That Evening, That Ride 🤍

🛵 One Scooter • One AirPod • A Few Songs • A Lot of Laughter • And You 🤍

"Some evenings are not just evenings. They quietly become memories that stay with you forever."

🛵

That day, we didn’t have much time. At around 6 PM, you came back from college, and I was already waiting for you downstairs with my scooter. The moment you came, we started our little adventure.

🎧 One AirPod in your ear, the other in mine.

"Hindi songs playing, both of us singing along, laughing, teasing people on the road, and enjoying every little moment. From Budhanilkantha to Maharajgunj and all the way to Lalitpur, it felt like the whole city was ours."

We visited Pimbahal Pokhari, and it was your first time seeing it at night. Then we stopped at Chiya Dailo in Ekantakuna for some tea and a croissant. We didn’t have much time, but somehow, every minute felt special.

Then came Jhamsikhel, the streets of Patan, and finally Thamel.

You had never seen Thamel at night before. The lights, the music, the busy streets, and the excitement on your face—I still remember it.

Of course, somewhere between all that happiness, we were also a little scared...

“Did your dad call?” “Did anyone find out?” “Do we need to go back?”

And before we knew it, it was already time to return.

We came back around 9 PM.

It was only a few hours, but somehow, those few hours became one of my favorite memories of us.

Because maybe it was never about how much time we had.

It was about who I had beside me.

One scooter.
One AirPod.
A few songs.
A lot of laughter.
And you.

"That was our little movie."

And no matter how many years pass,
I know I’ll always remember that evening. 🤍

For My Dearest TUDI

To My Dearest Rubina (My Forever TUDI ❤️),

Turning 18 is a big milestone, but to me, you have been my whole universe since the moment I saw you in your college dress walking down that road. One glance, and my heart decided your name.

I remember how guarded and scared you were on April 26 when we first talked. You told me: "k khasai kta manchey mathi alik barosha lagdaina kya... jhan cheat khayesi ta bho". I promised you that very morning that even if not boyfriend, I would be your truest friend, share your pains and joys, and make sure you never hurt again.

Look at us today. From you threatening "dumsi ko kapal bhutalera rakhdinchu" and calling me Goridas, to those late-night whispers where you said: "Kalidas pehele ka ho ya abhi ka hey toh Mera hi na" and "I just want these years to pass real quick so I can make you mine forever" — every single second with you has been pure magic.

That chilly 2.5-hour night ride across Kathmandu and Lalitpur, holding warm chiya at Chiya Dailo, watching the calm reflections at Pimbahal Pokhari, sharing one single pair of AirPods, singing into the cold wind while secretly praying your family wouldn't call... that wasn't just a scooter ride. That was when I knew my future has only one name: Rubina.

Today, as you step into 18, I make you this promise: Whichever Kalidas you have — old, new, silly, or grumpy — I am yours and only yours. I promise a lifetime of cold midnight rides, endless cups of sweet hot chiya, listening to your bossy "Chup!", and holding your hand through every high and low.

Happy 18th Birthday, my Tudi. You will always be my budi, my puntu, and my mutu.

Forever Yours,
Kalidas (Prakash) ❤️
